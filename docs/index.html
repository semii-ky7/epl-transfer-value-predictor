Market vs Model

A linear regression trained on 4,232 Premier League player-seasons (2012/13 to 2025/26) prices every outfield player from his stats alone: age, minutes, goals, assists, height and position. Search a player to see where it agrees with Transfermarkt, and where it doesn't.

    What the model learned

    The model is trained on the logarithm of market value, so each feature moves a player's price by a percentage rather than a fixed number of euros. Read each bar as "one more of this, everything else equal".

    Age beats goals

    Each extra year costs about 12% of a player's value. One more goal adds about 7%. Mohamed Salah scored 32 goals at 25 and 29 at 32; Transfermarkt valued him at €150m and then €50m.

    Stars are under-priced

    Players worth €100m or more come out at around €40–55m. Moisés Caicedo and Declan Rice score a handful of goals a season; their price is in defending, club and potential, none of which the model can see.

    Big seasons compound

    Because each goal multiplies value, 30 goals is roughly 7.6× the value of none. Haaland's 36-goal debut season comes out at €486m against Transfermarkt's €180m.

    How accurate is it?

    One in five player-seasons was held back during training. On those 847 unseen seasons, the typical miss is €5.4m and the average miss is €9.5m.

    Dots above the diagonal are players the model over-values; dots below are players it under-values. Cheap players sit above the line and expensive players sit below it: the model pulls everyone towards the middle. Select a dot to look that player up.

    Method

    1. Filter 1.9 million Transfermarkt appearances down to the Premier League: 149,751 rows.
    2. Build one row per player per season: games, minutes, goals, assists, age on 1 September, and the last market valuation of that season.
    3. Clean remove goalkeepers and seasons under 900 minutes, drop rows missing value or age, fill missing height with the median: 4,232 rows.
    4. Train linear regression on log(market value), 80/20 train/test split with a fixed seed.
    5. Publish predict every season with the saved model and export it for this page.

    The full write-up, code and decisions are in the GitHub repository. Market values come from the public Transfermarkt dataset on Kaggle compiled by David Cariboo. This project is not affiliated with Transfermarkt.
